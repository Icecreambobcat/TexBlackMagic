We are given that for a real quadratic $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and $a$, $b$, $c \in \mathbb{R}$ the following:
\[\begin{cases} f(a) = bc \\ f(b) = ac \\ f(c) = ab \end{cases}\]
\textit{ Sol. }
Let $f: x \mapsto kx^2 + qx + p$, for $k$, $q$, $p \in \mathbb{R}$.
Hence, we have the following:
\begin{align*}
	f(a) & = ka^2 + qa + p = bc \\
	f(b) & = kb^2 + qb + p = ac \\
	f(c) & = kc^2 + qc + p = ab
\end{align*}
Consider $f(a) - f(b) = k(a^2 - b^2) + q(a - b)$.
Hence:
\begin{align*}
  f(a) - f(b) &= (a-b)[k(a+b) + q]\\
  f(c) - f(a) &= (c-a)[k(a+c) + q]\\
  f(b) - f(c) &= (b-c)[k(b+c) + q]
\end{align*}
