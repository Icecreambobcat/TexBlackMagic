\section{Abstract} % 123
This report analyses engineering materials, including common ferrous and cuprous alloys, and engineered ceramics.
It covers manufacturing processes, applications, and compositions.
Specifically, the compositions of various ferrous metals utilised in automotive components and their properties are investigated to understand the justification for their application.
Surface treatments are also evaluated.
The report also outlines cuprous alloys, their composition, applications, and reasons for use in electrical applications.
It discusses forming methods for cuprous alloys and major engineering ceramics, their applications, and manufacturing processes.
Finally, it examines the validity of artificial intelligence-based proposals.

\section{Introduction} % 189
Engineered materials are ubiquitous, from everyday items to advanced technology.
This report examines case studies of these materials in real-life contexts, with diverse examples representing their diverse applications.
Ferrous alloys, commonly found in automotive components, are discussed, along with cuprous alloys used in electrical applications due to their synergistic characteristics.
Engineered ceramics, with granular control over properties and composition, are also explored.
The growing capabilities of artificial intelligence, however, begs the question: can engineers trust AI?
This report also seeks to answer this question, ultimately with the goal of presenting a high-level overview of engineered materials in context.
